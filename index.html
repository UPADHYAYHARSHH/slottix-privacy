Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 4 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Slottix collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information when you use the Slottix mobile application, website and related services.

1. About Slottix

Slottix ("Slottix", "we", "us", or "our") is a sports booking platform that helps users discover and book sports facilities, venues and activities in India.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the Slottix mobile application, website and other services that link to this Privacy Policy (collectively, the "Platform").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and Profile Information

When you create or maintain a Slottix account, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Password or account authentication information
  • Profile photograph
  • Date of birth

2.2 Location Information

Slottix uses location information to provide location-based sports discovery and booking functionality. Depending on your device settings and permissions, this may include your approximate or device-provided location.

Location information may be used to:

  • Show sports venues and activities relevant to your location
  • Help you find nearby sports facilities
  • Improve location-based features
  • Provide location-related functionality available through the Platform

You can manage location permissions through your device settings. If you disable required location access, some Slottix features may not work correctly.

2.3 Booking Information

When you search for or make a booking, we may collect:

  • Sport or activity selected
  • Venue or facility selected
  • Booking date and time
  • Booking duration and participant information where applicable
  • Booking amount
  • Booking status and booking/reference ID
  • Cancellation information

2.4 Slottix Coins

If you receive or use Slottix Coins, we may maintain records of your Coin balance and Coin transactions, including credits, redemptions, adjustments, expiry and the booking associated with a Coin transaction.

2.5 Payment and Transaction Information

Online payments on Slottix are facilitated through Cashfree Payments and applicable payment partners. We may receive transaction information needed to confirm and manage your booking, such as transaction/reference identifiers, amount, payment status and refund status.

We do not intend to store your complete card, bank-account or other sensitive payment credentials on Slottix's own systems where those details are handled by the applicable payment provider.

2.6 Device and Technical Information

We may collect technical and diagnostic information necessary to operate, secure and improve the Platform, such as device type, operating system, app version, IP address, network information, crash information, logs and other permitted device identifiers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create, authenticate and manage your Slottix account
  • Provide sports discovery and booking services
  • Process and confirm payments and bookings
  • Manage cancellations, refunds and Slottix Coins
  • Provide location-based features
  • Send booking confirmations, updates and important service notifications
  • Provide customer and technical support
  • Prevent fraud, misuse and unauthorized activity
  • Protect the security of the Platform
  • Improve the performance, reliability and functionality of Slottix
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

4. Legal Basis and Consent

Depending on the applicable law and the nature of the processing, Slottix may process personal information based on your consent, to provide services you request, to perform or administer transactions, to comply with legal obligations, or on another lawful basis available to Slottix.

Where consent is the basis for processing, you may withdraw consent through the available Slottix mechanism or by contacting us. Withdrawal of consent may affect our ability to provide features or services that depend on that information. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawfully completed before withdrawal or processing that may continue where permitted or required by applicable law.

5. Location Data

Location access is used to support Slottix's location-based sports discovery and booking experience. We do not sell your precise location information as a standalone product.

You may control location permissions through your device operating-system settings. Certain Slottix functionality may be unavailable if location access is disabled.

6. Payment Processing

Slottix uses Cashfree Payments to facilitate online payments. Payment information may be processed by Cashfree and relevant payment, banking or technology partners as necessary to process transactions, prevent fraud, comply with legal requirements and provide payment services.

Cashfree and other third parties may have their own terms and privacy policies. You should review those policies when using their services.

7. Sharing of Information

We may share personal information when reasonably necessary to provide and operate Slottix, including with:

  • Sports venue and facility operators, where necessary to fulfil a booking
  • Payment processors and financial-service providers
  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Analytics, diagnostics and technology service providers
  • Customer-support and communication providers
  • Security and fraud-prevention providers
  • Professional advisers and service providers
  • Government authorities, courts or law-enforcement agencies where required or permitted by law

We do not sell your personal information as a standalone product.

8. Sports Venue Operators

Slottix may connect users with independently operated sports venues and facilities. Information necessary to identify and fulfil a booking may be shared with the relevant venue operator.

Venue operators may have their own privacy practices and responsibilities regarding information they receive.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission, application or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for keeping your Slottix login credentials confidential and should contact us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain account and booking records, manage Slottix Coins, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, meet accounting or tax requirements, comply with applicable law, and protect the Platform.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, we may delete, anonymize or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with applicable requirements.
