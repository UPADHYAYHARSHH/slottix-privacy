8. Cancellation by the User

Eligible user cancellations may result in Slottix Coins based on the time remaining before the booked start time. The applicable percentage is calculated against the eligible booking amount according to the cancellation schedule below.

Cancellation time before booking Slottix Coins credited
24 hours or more 100% of eligible booking amount
12 hours to less than 24 hours 75% of eligible booking amount
3 hours to less than 12 hours 50% of eligible booking amount
Less than 3 hours 25% of eligible booking amount
No-show 0% of eligible booking amount

The exact cancellation eligibility and amount shown to you in the Slottix app at the time of cancellation will be the applicable cancellation result for that booking, subject to applicable law and any venue-specific terms disclosed before booking.
