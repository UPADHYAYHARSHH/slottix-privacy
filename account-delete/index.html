Delete your account from the app

You can delete your account directly inside the Slottix mobile app.

  1. Open the Slottix application.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Navigate to Profile or Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the deletion information and confirm your request.

Request deletion through email

You can start a deletion request without opening or reinstalling the app.

  • Send the request from your registered email address where possible.
  • Include the registered phone number on your account so we can verify the request.
  • We may ask for additional reasonable verification.
Never share your password, OTP or payment card details. Slottix support will never ask for them.

The button opens your default email app with the recipient, subject and a message already filled in. Add your details and send it.

Data and retention

Data that may be deleted

  • Account and profile information, such as name, email address, phone number and profile photograph
  • Date of birth and other profile details associated with the account
  • Location-related information associated with the account
  • Account-associated booking information, where eligible
  • Playora Coins records associated with the account
  • Other personal information associated with the account

Data that may be retained

Some information may need to be kept for legitimate legal, regulatory, financial, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution reasons, for example:

  • Transaction and payment records
  • Tax or accounting records
  • Information needed to resolve disputes
  • Records required by applicable law

Retained information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes. See Sections 10 and 11 of the Privacy Policy.

Important information

  • Account deletion may be irreversible.
  • You may lose access to your booking history and saved information.
  • Deletion does not automatically guarantee a refund for existing bookings. Refunds and Playora Coins remain governed by the Cancellation & Refund Policy.
  • You may need to settle pending obligations before deletion is completed, where legally permitted.
