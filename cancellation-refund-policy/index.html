Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains how booking cancellations, Slottix Coins, venue cancellations, payment failures and refunds are handled on Slottix.

1. Overview

Slottix allows users to book sports facilities, venues and activities. Depending on the circumstances and the time remaining before a booking, an eligible user cancellation may result in Slottix Coins rather than a cash refund.

Slottix Coins are booking credits that can be used toward eligible future bookings on Slottix, subject to the Coin rules described below.

2. User Cancellation Rules

If a user cancels an eligible confirmed booking, the amount of Slottix Coins credited is determined by the time remaining before the scheduled booking start time.

Time remaining before booking Slottix Coins credited
24 hours or more 100% of the eligible booking amount
12 hours to less than 24 hours 75% of the eligible booking amount
3 hours to less than 12 hours 50% of the eligible booking amount
Less than 3 hours 25% of the eligible booking amount
No-show 0% of the eligible booking amount

The cancellation result shown in the Slottix app at the time you request cancellation will be the applicable result for that booking, subject to applicable law and any venue-specific terms disclosed before booking.

3. Examples

Example 1 — Early Cancellation

Eligible booking amount: ₹1,000. Cancellation is made 30 hours before the booking. The user receives 1,000 Slottix Coins.

Example 2 — Medium Cancellation

Eligible booking amount: ₹1,000. Cancellation is made 18 hours before the booking. The user receives 750 Slottix Coins.

Example 3 — Late Cancellation

Eligible booking amount: ₹1,000. Cancellation is made 6 hours before the booking. The user receives 500 Slottix Coins.

Example 4 — Last-Minute Cancellation

Eligible booking amount: ₹1,000. Cancellation is made 2 hours before the booking. The user receives 250 Slottix Coins.

4. No-Show

If a user does not attend a confirmed booking and does not complete an eligible cancellation before the booking start time, the booking will generally be treated as a no-show.

No-show bookings receive 0% Slottix Coins, subject to applicable law or any specific exception communicated by Slottix.

5. Cancellation by a Venue or Owner

If a venue or sports facility owner cancels a confirmed booking, is unable to provide the booked service, or makes the booked facility unavailable, the affected user will generally receive 100% of the eligible booking amount as Slottix Coins.

The user will not be placed into a lower cancellation tier because of a cancellation caused by the venue.

Slottix may also provide an alternative resolution, such as rescheduling or another appropriate remedy, depending on the circumstances and applicable law.

6. Cancellation by Slottix

If Slottix cancels a confirmed booking for reasons attributable to Slottix or the Platform, the affected user will generally receive 100% of the eligible booking amount as Slottix Coins.

Where a cash refund or another remedy is required by applicable law, payment rules or the specific circumstances of the transaction, Slottix may provide that remedy instead.

7. Slottix Coins

7.1 Value

Unless otherwise stated for a particular offer, 1 Slottix Coin has a redemption value of ₹1 toward eligible Slottix bookings.

7.2 Validity

Slottix Coins credited under the standard cancellation policy are valid for 60 days from the date of credit, unless a different validity period is explicitly communicated for a specific Coin grant.

7.3 Maximum Redemption

A maximum of 40% of an eligible booking amount may be paid using Slottix Coins.

Example:

If an eligible booking costs ₹1,000, a maximum of ₹400 can be paid using Slottix Coins. The remaining ₹600 must be paid using an available payment method.

7.4 Remaining Coin Balance

If the user has more Coins than the maximum amount that can be redeemed for a particular booking, the unused Coins remain in the user's account subject to their individual expiry dates and other applicable rules.

7.5 Coin Restrictions

  • Slottix Coins are intended for eligible Slottix bookings.
  • Slottix Coins cannot normally be transferred between user accounts.
  • Slottix Coins cannot normally be sold or exchanged for cash.
  • Slottix Coins cannot be used to purchase additional Slottix Coins.
  • Expired Coins cannot normally be redeemed.
  • Slottix may reverse Coins issued due to fraud, abuse, technical error or unauthorized activity.

8. Cash Refunds

The standard Slottix user-cancellation mechanism is Slottix Coins rather than a cash refund.

However, a cash refund may apply where a payment was completed but a booking was not successfully confirmed, where a duplicate payment occurred, where Slottix or a venue determines that a cash refund is appropriate, or where a cash refund or other remedy is required by applicable law or payment rules.

9. Payment Deducted but Booking Failed

If money is deducted from your payment method but Slottix does not successfully confirm your booking, please contact Slottix Support with the transaction details.

Slottix will investigate the transaction. Where a refund is due, the applicable refund will generally be processed through the relevant payment mechanism and may be returned to the original payment method, subject to payment-provider and banking timelines.

10. Duplicate Payment

If you believe you were charged more than once for the same booking, contact Slottix Support as soon as possible.

Please provide your booking ID, registered email address, payment amount and transaction or reference ID so that we can investigate.

11. Refund Processing Time

Where a cash refund is approved, the time required for the amount to appear in your bank account, card, UPI account or other payment method may depend on the payment provider, bank, card issuer or financial institution.

Slottix does not control the processing time after a refund has been successfully initiated with the relevant payment provider.

12. Venue Responsibility

Venue operators are responsible for maintaining the availability of their facilities and honouring confirmed bookings according to their arrangements with Slottix.

Slottix may take appropriate action against venues that repeatedly cancel confirmed bookings, provide inaccurate availability or otherwise fail to fulfil their obligations.

13. Changes to Cancellation Rules

Slottix may update its cancellation, Coin or refund rules from time to time. The cancellation terms applicable to a booking will be shown or communicated to the user as required before the booking is completed.

Changes to this policy will be published with an updated "Last Updated" date.

14. Consumer Rights and Applicable Law

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude, restrict or waive any consumer right, refund, remedy or protection that cannot legally be excluded or restricted under applicable law.

Where applicable law requires a different remedy from the standard Slottix Coins process, Slottix will follow the applicable legal requirement.
